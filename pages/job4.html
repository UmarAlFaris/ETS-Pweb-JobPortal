Community Manager - Zenlesss Zone Zero
Category: Manager
Location: Surabaya
Company: Hoyoverse.

Job Description

We are looking for a passionate Community Manager to join our team for the exciting project, Zenlesss Zone Zero. As a Community Manager, you will be responsible for building and nurturing our gaming community, engaging with players, and creating a positive and inclusive environment for our users.

Responsibilities include:

  • Develop and implement community engagement strategies.
  • Moderate online forums and social media channels.
  • Organize community events and activities.
  • Collaborate with the development team to provide player feedback.
  • Monitor and report on community sentiment and trends.
